# Project Workflow & Guidelines

## Weekly Rhythm

- **Monday-Friday:** Monitor sources continuously
- **Friday/Saturday:** Compile main news items
- **Sunday/Monday:** Publish weekly agenda
- **Monday:** Archive and begin new week

## Source Monitoring Checklist

Each week, verify these sources:

- [ ] Infoydata (daily check)
- [ ] Página/12 labour section
- [ ] La Izquierda Diario
- [ ] CGT official communications
- [ ] CTA Autónoma communications
- [ ] Key union Twitter/X accounts
- [ ] Ministry of Labour announcements
- [ ] INDEC economic data releases
- [ ] International labour news (ILO, regional)

## Content Categories

**Strikes & Labour Actions**
- Strike announcements and updates
- Picket lines and protests
- Labour conflicts

**Wage Negotiations**
- Collective bargaining updates
- Salary agreements
- Cost of living adjustments

**Policy & Legislation**
- Government labour initiatives
- Legal changes
- Regulatory updates

**Economic Data**
- Inflation rates
- Wage indices
- Employment figures

**International & Regional**
- Latin American labour news
- Global labour movements
- Comparative analysis

## Quality Guidelines

- **Accuracy:** Cross-reference major stories with multiple sources
- **Timeliness:** Include recent developments (last 7 days)
- **Diversity:** Include different ideological perspectives
- **Attribution:** Always link to original sources
- **Neutrality:** Present facts; flag analysis separately
