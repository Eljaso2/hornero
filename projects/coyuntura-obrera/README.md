# Coyuntura Obrera - Labour News Agency

Weekly news aggregation on labour, workers, and unions in Argentina and Latin America.

## Project Structure

- **sources/** - Curated media sources catalog
- **weekly-agendas/** - Weekly news compilations and agendas
- **archive/** - Historical agendas and reports

## Mission

Aggregate and synthesize news from:
- Press outlets (investigative journalism, economic press)
- Union websites and official communications
- Social media (X, Facebook, Instagram)
- Labour movement publications
- Official government labour ministry sources

## Weekly Workflow

1. Monitor sources throughout the week
2. Compile agenda by Sunday/Monday
3. Organize by theme: strikes, negotiations, policy, international
4. Archive and analyze trends
