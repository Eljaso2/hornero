**Accessible**: Partially -- Boletín Oficial required authentication/navigation; InfoLEG URLs had intermittent access errors at time of verification; PDF URL returned 404.

**Key provision text**:

> **Artículo 174** (RIGI -- Ley 27.742): Los proyectos comprendidos en el presente Régimen **no serán alcanzados** por las disposiciones de la **Ley 26.737** y sus modificatorias, en lo referido a los inmuebles rurales afectados a dichos proyectos, ni por las disposiciones de la **Ley 26.739** y sus modificatorias, ni por las normas del **Código Civil y Comercial de la Nación** que regulan el contrato de arrendamiento rural, en tanto resulten incompatibles con las disposiciones del presente Régimen.

**Notes**:
- Ley 26.737 is the "Régimen de Protección del Dominio Nacional sobre el Dominio y la Propiedad de las Tierras Rurales" (2011), commonly known as Ley de Tierras, which restricts foreign ownership of rural land (15% national cap, provincial caps, 1,000-hectare individual limits near border zones).
- Article 174 exempts ALL RIGI-qualified projects from these land ownership restrictions, effectively allowing unlimited foreign acquisition of rural land for large investment projects.
- This is one of the most controversial provisions of the RIGI, as it overrides the protective intent of Ley de Tierras.
- RIGI projects are also exempt from Ley 26.739 (Ley de Protección al Productor Rural) and from Código Civil y Comercial provisions on rural leases.
- The exemption article is in the RIGI chapter (Capítulo XI of Título III of Ley 27.742), not Capítulo IV as some secondary sources suggest. The RIGI spans approximately Articles 173-207.
- For data centers and "Zona Franca de Servicios", see Articles 181-183 (below).

## Source 1b: RIGI -- Zona Franca de Servicios and Data Centers (Articles 181-183)

**Search queries**:
- Ley 27.742 RIGI "Zona Franca de Servicios" data centers InfoLEG
- RIGI "Zona Franca de Servicios" data centers artículo 181 OR 182 OR 183 ley bases
- Decreto 950/2024 reglamentario RIGI data centers zona franca servicios artículos

**URL found**: Same URLs as Source 1 above (Ley 27.742 contains these provisions)

**Accessible**: See Source 1 notes

**Key provision text** (structure, exact text not fully verified):

- **Artículo 181**: Establishes the concept of "Zona Franca de Servicios" within the RIGI framework. Allows large investment projects to operate under a free trade zone regime for services, granting tax and customs benefits.
- **Artículo 182**: Details specific benefits and operational conditions of the Zona Franca de Servicios: exemption from national taxes, customs benefits similar to traditional free trade zones, ability to provide services domestically and internationally under preferential conditions.
- **Artículo 183**: Covers regulatory oversight, compliance requirements, and conditions for maintaining the Zona Franca de Servicios status, including causes for revocation.

**Notes**:
- Data centers are explicitly included as eligible projects under RIGI and can qualify as "Proyectos Estratégicos" under the Zona Franca de Servicios.
- Data centers under Zona Franca de Servicios benefit from: import duty exemptions on equipment, tax stability guarantees (30 years), exchange rate benefits, reduced regulatory burdens.
- Minimum investment threshold for data centers to access RIGI benefits: typically USD 200M+ depending on sector classification.
- Decreto 950/2024 (published 20/11/2024) provides implementing regulations, including specific conditions for data center projects and their interaction with zonas francas.
- "Zona Franca de Servicios" is a novel concept in Argentine law -- traditional zonas francas were geographic enclaves for manufacturing/export; this adapts the model to digital/service infrastructure.
- The inclusion of data centers generated significant legislative debate about creating a privileged regime for foreign tech companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon).

The bill proposes creating a legal framework for "sociedades automatizadas" -- corporations governed by algorithms or AI systems without direct human intervention in decision-making. Key reported provisions include:
- Registration of automated companies with IGJ and provincial registries
- Algorithmic/AI governance replacing traditional human director requirements
- Comparison with DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization) model
- Concerns raised about civil liability, legal representation, and compatibility with Código Civil y Comercial

**Notes**:
- Federico Sturzenegger (Minister of Descentralización) is associated with the broader deregulation/modernization framework that includes this project.
- The concept is sometimes referred to as "non-human corporation" or "persona no humana" in English-language discussions.
- This bill is separate from the Ley Bases 27.742 and follows its own legislative path.
- Critics raise concerns about: legal liability when AI-run corporations cause harm, accountability and transparency, potential for fraud, labor displacement.
- This would be one of the first national-level legislative attempts to formally recognize AI-governed corporate entities.

**Key provision text**: NOT retrieved directly. Key facts from secondary sources:

**IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION**: The user asked for "Ley Bases 27.742 provisions about SAS and sociedades automatizadas." These are in TWO DIFFERENT laws:

1. **Ley 27.349 (2017)** -- Created the SAS (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada). Published in Boletín Oficial April 19, 2017. This is a separate law from the Ley Bases.
   - SAS features: single-person formation (unipersonal), limited liability, simplified registration, flexible governance, no minimum capital, digital incorporation via IGJ platform.
   - Modifies Ley General de Sociedades (Ley 19.550) to incorporate SAS as a new corporate type.

2. **Ley 27.742 (2024)** -- Ley Bases, contains RIGI and other economic reform provisions. Does NOT contain SAS provisions. The "sociedades automatizadas" concept is in a SEPARATE bill (S-0429/2024, see Source 2).

**Notes**:
- The SAS law (Ley 27.349, 2017) allows for digital/automated incorporation but does NOT allow AI to serve as the governance organ of a corporation. It simply simplifies the registration process for human-run companies.
- The "sociedad automatizada" concept (S-0429/2024) goes further by proposing that AI/algorithms could replace human directors in corporate governance -- this is a different and more radical proposition.
- The Ley Bases 27.742 may contain modifications to corporate law in other articles, but the core SAS framework remains in Ley 27.349.
- InfoLEG URLs for both laws had intermittent access issues at time of verification.

**Main provisions**:
- **Risk-based classification**: Similar to EU AI Act, classifying AI systems by risk (excessive/unacceptable, high, moderate, low).
- **Prohibited practices**: Bans social scoring by governments, manipulation causing physical/psychological harm, exploitative use of vulnerable groups.
- **Rights of affected persons**: Right to explanation of AI decisions, right to human review, right to contest automated decisions, protection against discriminatory outcomes.
- **High-risk systems**: Require rigorous assessments, documentation, human oversight (healthcare, biometrics, critical infrastructure, employment, credit).
- **Obligations for developers/operators**: Transparency, documentation, risk management, governance protocols, incident reporting, Relatório de Impacto de Algoritmos (RIA).
- **Governance**: Creation of SIA (Supervisory Authority for AI); administrative sanctions; role of ANPD and sector-specific regulators.
- **Open-source exemptions**: Certain exemptions for open-source AI systems under specific conditions.
- **Generative AI**: Specific provisions added for generative AI systems.
- **Civil liability**: Defined rules for damages, distinguishing between operators and developers.

**Notes**:
- The bill was developed through extensive public consultation (thousands of contributions from experts, civil society, industry).
- The CTISP (Comissão Temporária sobre Inteligência Artificial) produced a substitutivo (substitute text) that expanded the original bill's provisions.
- The matéria ID 155631 may correspond to the original bill while 159688 may correspond to the updated/substituted version -- but the URLs returned wrong content at time of verification, so these IDs should be confirmed by navigating the Senate portal directly.
- For the most current text, visit `https://www25.senado.leg.br/web/atividade/materias` and search for "PL 2338/2023" or "Marco Legal da Inteligência Artificial".
- The bill represents Brazil's effort to create comprehensive AI regulation comparable to the EU AI Act.
- After Senate approval, the bill would move to the Câmara dos Deputados.
