# Marx sobre las Compañías de las Indias Orientales — Referencias primarias

## SRC-MARX-01: VOC holandesa — charter y soberanía

**Entidad**: Vereenigde Oost-Indische Compagnie (VOC) — Compañía Holandesa de las Indias Orientales (1602)
**Distinción**: La VOC no es la EIC inglesa (1600, India) ni la WIC holandesa (1621, Caribe/América). Milei se refiere explícitamente a la VOC (1602). Marx analizó extensamente la EIC inglesa.
**Charter**: Staten-Generaal (parlamento holandés) otorgó poder de guerra, tratados, conquista, tributo, justicia, ejército y navy — soberanía plena desde charter original, no gradual como la EIC inglesa.
**Territorio**: Indonesia (Java, Molucas, Sumatra), Sri Lanka, Sudáfrica (Cape Colony)
**Amsterdam**: Centro financiero global gracias al saqueo colonial VOC — primer mercado bursátil (Amsterdam Stock Exchange, 1602), primera empresa cotizante con responsabilidad limitada

## SRC-MARX-02: Marx artículos NYT sobre la EIC inglesa

**"The East India Company — Its History and Results"** (11 julio 1853, NYT)
URL: https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1853/07/11.htm

Key quotes:
- *"transformed the East India Company from a commercial into a military and territorial power"*
- *"the British Government has been fighting, under the Company's name, for two centuries"*
- *"The East India Company excluded the common people from the commerce with India, at the same time that the House of Commons excluded them from the Parliamentary representation"*
- *"the Indian monopolists were the first preachers of free trade in England"*
- *"the treasures transported from India to England were gained much less by comparatively insignificant commerce, than by the direct exploitation of that country"*
- *"even as early as 1689, they had conceived the establishment of a dominion in India, and of making territorial revenue one of their sources of emolument"*
- *"the Company and its employés from 1757-1766 got £6,000,000 from the Indians as gifts"*
- *"The power the East India Company had obtained by bribing the Government…it was forced to maintain by bribing again"*
- *"that era of apparent liberty was in reality the era of monopolies not created by Royal grants…but authorized and nationalized by the sanction of Parliament"*
- *"the interests of the moneyocracy which had converted India into its landed estates, of the oligarchy who had conquered it by their armies, and of the millocracy who had inundated it with their fabrics, had gone hand in hand"*

## SRC-MARX-03: Marx sobre monopolistas como predicadores del libre comercio

Fuente: "The East India Company — Its History and Results" (1853)
Quote: *"the Indian monopolists were the first preachers of free trade in England"*
Relevancia: Los monopolistas charter defendían su monopolio en el lenguaje de libertad comercial — Milei hace lo mismo: defender desregulación (= monopolio de capital tech) como "libertad".

## SRC-MARX-04: Marx, El Capital Vol. I, Capítulo 31 — Acumulación originaria

URL: https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch31.htm

Key quotes:
- *"The English East India Company, as is well known, obtained, besides the political rule in India, the exclusive monopoly of the tea-trade"*
- *"The monopolies of salt, opium, betel and other commodities, were inexhaustible mines of wealth"* — *"The employés themselves fixed the price and plundered at will the unhappy Hindus"*
- *"the Company and its employés from 1757-1766 got £6,000,000 from the Indians as gifts"* — *"fortunes sprang up like mushrooms in a day; primitive accumulation went on without the advance of a shilling"*
- *"the English manufactured a famine by buying up all the rice and refusing to sell it again"*
- *"Holland, which first fully developed the colonial system…was the head capitalistic nation of the 17th century"* — BUT *"the people of Holland were more over-worked, poorer and more brutally oppressed than those of all the rest of Europe"*
- *"its colonial administration was one of the most extraordinary relations of treachery, bribery, massacre, and meanness"* — *"Sweet commerce!"*
- *"The colonial system ripened, like a hot-house, trade and navigation"*
- *"The colonies secured a market for the budding manufactures, and, through the monopoly of the market, an increased accumulation"*
- *"The treasures captured outside Europe by undisguised looting, enslavement, and murder, floated back to the mother-country and were there turned into capital"*
- *"Force is the midwife of every old society pregnant with a new one. It is itself an economic power"*
- *"capital comes dripping from head to foot, from every pore, with blood and dirt"*
