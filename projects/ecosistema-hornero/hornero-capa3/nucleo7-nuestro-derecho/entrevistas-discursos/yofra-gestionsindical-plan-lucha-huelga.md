# Daniel Yofra (Aceiteros): reclamó un plan de lucha con huelga contra la reforma laboral

**Gestión Sindical — 22/12/2025**

---

## Plan de lucha total

Yofra demanded a **total confrontation plan** against the Milei government. Expressed hope that the CGT mobilization would serve as the starting point for a sustained action agenda: **"Ojalá la movilización de la CGT haya sido el punto de partida para un verdadero plan de lucha."**

All labor central organizations and unions must unite forces and coordinate ongoing action against the libertarian attack described as **"directo y regresivo"** against acquired rights.

## Huelga, no solo diálogo

**"No va a alcanzar con el diálogo ni con una movilización. Hay que hacer huelga."** This aligns with other union voices like ATE calling for coordinated actions so the labor modernization project is ultimately defeated.

## Gobierno declaró la guerra

**"Es un gobierno que llegó para hacerle la guerra a la clase trabajadora y a los jubilados."** The executive branch's labor reform seeks to roll back labor rights by 200 years. The union is already holding assemblies at workplaces to raise awareness.

## Defensa de la organización sindical

**"Sin organización sindical y sin la huelga como herramienta de lucha, nunca hubíeramos logrado salarios que nos permitan vivir dignamente."** The reform removes rights and aims to destroy union organizations — the structures that enabled current achievements.

## Elecciones sindicales — Lista Azul

Presentation of Lista Azul on December 17 (deadline for officialization). With only one list submitted, Lista Azul was officially proclaimed. Elections scheduled for **March 5, 2026**, through direct and secret voting of affiliates, with 51 ballot boxes distributed nationwide.

Yofra committed to defending: Salario Mínimo Vital y Móvil, deepening of union democracy, Mixed Committees, and every labor right achieved over 20 years.

## Referencia: acuerdo aceitero tras huelga

The Aceiteros agreement that forced Minister Caputo's hand was reached after a **seven-day national strike**.
