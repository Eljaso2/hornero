### 02. Milei como Promoter del dato-exportismo

**Sub-thesis**: Milei no "libera" — abre el campo al capital tech extranjero. La desregulación no es libertad — es vulnerabilidad.
**Hook**: *"La IA que Milei promete es la IA que Thiel necesita — y Argentina es la data farm, no Amsterdam"*
**Content requirements**:
- Milei+Sturzenegger FT article: three pillars (IA desregulada, corporación no humana, ambiente fiscal)
- The Amsterdam analogy: Milei wants Buenos Aires = Amsterdam of IA. But Amsterdam was the center of a colonial extraction company. Argentina = the colony, not Amsterdam.
- Thiel connection: Thiel in Buenos Aires weeks before the project, DAOs match Thiel's investments
- Trump contrast: even Trump's executive order has some oversight — Argentina rejects all
- End transitioning to Palantir as the concrete case

**Key sources needed**: milei-ft.md (full), Infobae coverage, Trump executive order, Thiel-Milei timeline

### 03. Palantir = La Forestal digital

**Sub-thesis**: Palantir es la nueva La Forestal — empresa extranjera que territorializa recursos, extrae sin accountability, y el territorio queda sin soberanía.
**Hook**: *"Data centers son las nuevas fábricas del imperialismo — su producto no es tanino, es datos"*
**Content requirements**:
- Palantir contract: access to ANSES + ARCA data, negotiated without congressional oversight
- CELS/ADC/Via Libre objections: violation Ley 25.326, mass surveillance, data sovereignty
- The Forestal analogy developed: British company territorializes Chaco → Thiel company territorializes Argentine data
- "Europa frena Palantir" (Magnani, Página/12): Europe rejects Palantir by GDPR; Argentina embraces it
- Moyano Czertok's tríada: Argentina loses sovereignty over datos + algoritmos + hardware simultaneously
- End transitioning to tierras/data centers

**Key sources needed**: Página/12 Palantir articles, CELS objections, Magnani "Europa frena Palantir", Moyano Czertok tríada

### 04. Tierras para Data Centers

**Sub-thesis**: RIGI exime data centers de Ley de Tierras. Zonas francas = enclaves territoriales. Patagonia como nueva zona de sacrificio.
**Hook**: *"Como La Forestal ocupó el Chaco para extraer tanino, hoy Thiel busca tierras patagónicas para extraer datos"*
**Content requirements**:
- Ley 26.737 (Tierras): limits foreign ownership 15%, restricts border zones
- RIGI modifications: data center projects exempt from Ley 26.737
- Zonas Francas de Servicios: new category, data centers operate with limited Argentine law
- Patagonia as location: Chubut, Río Negro — cold climate, renewable energy, strategic territory
- Página/12 "No se puede ser tan antipatria" — inviolabilidad de propiedad privada enables strategic territory sale
- USD 2+ billion in data center proposals
- End transitioning to sociedades automatizadas

**Key sources needed**: Ley 26.737, RIGI text, Página/12 "No se puede ser tan antipatria", Infobae/Ambito data center proposals

### 05. Sociedades Automatizadas: capitalismo post-humano

**Sub-thesis**: Empresas sin humanos = capital sin accountability. La "corporación no humana" es la forma pura del capital — valor que se auto-reproduce sin trabajo vivo.

- Milei's 1602 analogy: Dutch East India Company = colonial extraction enterprise, not innovation
- The question: ¿Argentina es Amsterdam o Batavia?
- End transitioning to desinformación

**Key sources needed**: milei-ft.md (pilar 2, DAOs, Sturzenegger 50M agents), Ley Bases 27.742, DNU 70/2023

### 07. Brasil: dato-industrialización soberana

**Sub-thesis**: Brasil no es solo "más regulado" — está intentando industrializar los datos. Soberanía sobre la tríada: datos + algoritmos + hardware.
**Hook**: *"Brasil construye soberanía digital; Argentina la cede"*
**Content requirements**:
- PL 2338/2023 (Marco Legal da IA): risk-based framework, approved by Senate, now in Câmara

- ANPD: supervisory authority = control over the tríada
- Prohibitions: social scoring, subliminal manipulation, mass biometrics
- Controversies: industry-friendly tilt in final Senate text, Access Now criticism
- Brazil attempting dato-industrialización: process data locally, generate sovereignty
- Contrast: Argentina opens the basket (desregulación) for foreign extraction
- End transitioning to resistance

**Key sources needed**: PL 2338/2023, LGPD, Access Now criticism, ANPD

## Format Specifications

### Headings
- Level-1 heading (#): Used only once, for the document title at the top
- Level-2 heading (##): Each section heading
- Level-3 heading (###): Sub-sections within a section
- No heading level below ### in the newsletter body

### Citations
- In-text: [SRC-XXX] format, e.g., "Según [SRC-004], Milei se reunió con Thiel..."
- Direct quotes: Use `>` block format with attribution
- At end: Full References section expanding all SRC-XXX to complete source info

### Lists
- Blank line before and after all lists
- Use unordered lists (-) for enumerations
- Use ordered lists (1.) only for sequential processes

### Tables
- Standard Markdown table format
- Blank line before and after tables
- Tables should be adapted and narratively integrated, not just pasted

### Blank Lines
- One blank line at beginning of each section file
- One blank line at end of each section file
- Blank line before and after all block elements (headings, lists, quotes, tables, code blocks)

### Word Count Targets
- Per section: 800-1200 words
- Total newsletter: 8,000-12,000 words (excluding References section)

### Language
- Spanish (español)
- No mixing of English terms without explanation
- Technical terms (RAG, DAO, LLM) may be used but should be briefly explained in context for non-technical readers
