# Fact-Check Report: Tricontinental Newsletter — IA en America Latina

## Executive Summary

This report covers the fact-check verification of 59 claims extracted from the Tricontinental Newsletter on IA in America Latina. Claims were verified against research materials (6 source files) and web sources.

### Verdict Breakdown

| Verdict | Count | Percentage |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| ✓ CONFIRMED | 41 | 69% |
| ◐ PARTIAL | 6 | 10% |
| ✗ CONTRADICTED | 0 | 0% |
| ? UNVERIFIABLE | 6 | 10% |
| ⚠ NEEDS_SOURCE (unresolved) | 0 | 0% |
| LOW/Excluded | 1 | 2% |
| Editorial/Interpretive (not strictly verifiable) | 5 | 8% |

**Key findings**: No claims were contradicted by sources. The newsletter's factual core is well-supported. Six PARTIAL claims involve nuances in wording (attribution chains, editorial characterization, or specific superlatives). Six claims remain UNVERIFIABLE (mostly World Cup opening-paragraph context claims where press coverage was not found). One claim (CLM-007, Malvinas banner) remains UNVERIFIABLE despite the underlying match result being confirmed.

**Overall assessment**: The newsletter's substantive content about Thiel/Milei/IA/land law/DAOs/VOC/Marx is well-sourced and accurate. The opening paragraphs about the World Cup contain confirmed facts (Spain won, Argentina defeated England, Venezuela earthquake) but one specific claim (Malvinas banner display by players) could not be verified.
